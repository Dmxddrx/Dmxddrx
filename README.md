## About Me

My engineering background is in **Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering Technology** at the **Institute of Technology, University of Moratuwa**. I enjoy working close to the hardware layer and developing complete systems that combine electronics, firmware, sensing, and software.

My main interests are:

- **Embedded systems:** microcontroller firmware, peripheral drivers, communication protocols, DMA, timers, and real-time hardware control.
- **Robotics and motion control:** motor control, feedback systems, IMU integration, LiDAR interfacing, and autonomous-robot subsystems.
- **Electronics and PCB design:** circuit development, hardware integration, testing, and debugging.
- **IoT applications:** Wi-Fi-connected sensor nodes, local databases, server-side data handling, and monitoring dashboards.

I focus on understanding how the complete system behaves—not just getting individual components to work.

## Featured Projects

### 1. Autonomous Floor-Cleaning Robot

**STM32F407VET6 · Embedded C · Raspberry Pi 4B · LiDAR · Motor Control**

Developing the embedded control system for a product-oriented autonomous floor-cleaning robot. The STM32F407 handles low-level hardware control and real-time operations, while a Raspberry Pi 4B is used for mapping. Work includes motor-driver control, sensor integration, UART/DMA communication, and closed-loop speed regulation. LiDAR motor RPM control and system-level tuning are ongoing development areas.

### 2. ClimateNode_360 — IoT Environmental Monitoring

**ESP32-C3 · SHT30 · GP2Y1010 · ADS1115 · PHP · MySQL · Chart.js**

A distributed environmental monitoring system that collects temperature, humidity, and airborne-dust measurements from ESP32-C3 sensor nodes over a local Wi-Fi network. Data is sent to a local XAMPP server, stored in MySQL, and presented through a PHP-based dashboard with live readings and historical charts.

Firmware includes periodic sampling and averaging, sensor-bus coordination during Wi-Fi communication, watchdog-based recovery, and controlled dust-sensor operation.

[View ClimateNode_360 on GitHub](https://github.com/Dmxddrx/ClimateNode-360)

### 3. ESP32-C3 Water Matrix Clock

**ESP32-C3 · MAX7219 · SSD1306 OLED · MPU6050 · Wi-Fi · Bluetooth**

An embedded clock built around two cascaded 8×8 MAX7219 LED matrices. It combines network time synchronization with an OLED status interface, MPU6050-based orientation detection, and an animated sand-clock display. The firmware also includes device-status handling and battery/charging monitoring.

[View the Water Matrix Clock repository](https://github.com/Dmxddrx/ESP32-C3-Water-Matrix-Clock)

### 4. Gesture-Controlled Mecanum Vehicle

**STM32F103C8T6 · Mecanum Drive · MPU9250 · nRF24L01**

An omnidirectional Mecanum-wheel vehicle controlled through a motion-sensing hand interface. The project explores gesture input, wireless communication, and motor-vector control for forward, lateral, diagonal, and rotational movement.

### 5. Smart Waste Management System

**Arduino · Sensors · Motorized Mechanisms**

Designed a multi-stage waste-sorting system that identifies and separates dry, wet, and metallic materials using sensor inputs and automated actuators.
